## SERARA usage

This asset is a Phase 01 engineering and visual-development body.

It replaces the procedural capsule mannequin because Phase 01 needs:
- continuous humanoid skin
- an actual skeleton
- real skinned deformation
- readable body proportions
- animation clips
- a geometry base that can accept SERARA material/state work

It is NOT the final SERARA character identity.

We override its runtime material with SERARA porcelain/mineral material and will later replace/refine the mesh with a custom canonical sculpt while preserving the rig/state/perception architecture.
