## Canonical character law

SERARA is not:
- a puppet;
- an avatar;
- a motion-capture mirror;
- a webcam demo;
- a game NPC;
- a list of gesture commands.

SERARA is an embodied digital presence whose body, face, material, chamber, light, sound and memory participate in one encounter.

The camera is a sensory organ.

Visitor signals are interpreted before response.

## Closed interaction architecture

### Experiment 01 — Attention Memory

Accepted:
- delayed attention;
- hesitant gaze;
- stillness as event;
- recognition through held presence;
- avoidance under fast movement;
- encounter afterimage.

### Experiment 02 — Semantic Camera Perception

Accepted:
- local browser camera input;
- face + pose semantic extraction;
- proximity;
- movement energy;
- stillness;
- smile;
- openness;
- shoulder asymmetry;
- raised-hand salience;
- approximate head yaw / roll.

SERARA does not mirror these signals directly.

### Experiment 03 — Embodied Performance Conductor

Accepted performance grammar:

`DORMANT -> NOTICE -> ATTUNE / STRAIN -> FRACTURE -> AFTERMATH -> REFORM`

The performance is branching.

A calm visitor can remain in ATTUNE without ever causing FRACTURE.

Disturbance accumulates through STRAIN before rupture.

Body, face, chamber camera, lighting, fire / fragments, floor field and sound read the same authored phase.

A click/touch is pressure + wake only.

A click does not directly fire the rupture.

The cinematic burst is emitted on the actual transition into FRACTURE.

### Experiment 04 — Camera Embodied Readability

Triggered by real-device video review.

The recording proved that the camera was technically active and producing strong semantic values while the visible body response remained underweighted.

Accepted correction:
- camera authority latches once real perception becomes active;
- raw pointer becomes fallback, not competing authority;
- stronger bounded head / neck orientation;
- torso / shoulder participation;
- proximity negotiation;
- approach recoil;
- pre-fracture brace under high motion;
- more legible recognition-conditioned warmth;
- more readable internal signal response.

The camera proof deliberately moves the raw pointer against the camera signal and requires camera authority to remain active.

## Emotional contract

This contract prevents endless aesthetic tuning.

### DORMANT / FIRST ENCOUNTER

Target:
- curiosity;
- restrained awe;
- uncertainty.

Not target:
- immediate horror;
- constant suffocation.

### NOTICE

Target:
- the visitor feels observed;
- recognition is uncertain;
- attention has weight.

Not target:
- instant reward;
- obvious tracking demo.

### ATTUNE

Target:
- strange intimacy;
- cautious connection;
- quieter room;
- reduced incidental motion.

Not target:
- strong suffocation;
- panic;
- theatrical escalation.

### STRAIN

Target:
- pressure;
- resistance;
- growing unease;
- bodily brace.

Not target:
- full rupture immediately;
- calm neutrality.

### FRACTURE

Target:
- concentrated shock;
- rupture;
- temporary psychological pressure.

The strongest oppressive feeling is allowed here because it is a dramatic peak, not the baseline state.

### AFTERMATH

Target:
- consequence;
- exhaustion;
- weight;
- residue.

Not target:
- instant reset;
- new spectacle.

### REFORM

Target:
- release;
- uneasy calm;
- melancholic recovery;
- the sense that the same being has returned altered by the encounter.

Not target:
- identical emotional intensity to FRACTURE.

## Final acceptance rule

Future tuning is justified only if one of these is broken:
- a phase produces the wrong emotional function;
- camera authority regresses;
- body / world disagree;
- interaction reads as dropped-frame lag rather than intention;
- performance becomes puppet-like;
- a technical regression appears.

Personal moment-to-moment taste alone is not a reason to reopen the system.
